About PFA

Pen Fight Association is a dedicated organization built around the sport, competition and community of pen fighting.

Our Mission


Our mission is to create a fair, organized and enjoyable environment where pen fighters can compete, improve their skills and connect with one another.

Our Vision


We want to build a strong pen fighting community where talent, sportsmanship and friendship come together.

PFA Values

⚖️ Fair Play

Every competitor deserves fair treatment.

🤝 Respect

Respect opponents, teammates and organizers.

🏆 Excellence

Always try to improve your skills.
